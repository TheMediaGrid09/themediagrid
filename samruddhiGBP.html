The Challenge

3 Years on Google. Still Outside the Top 20.

Samruddhi T-Shirts had already been present on Google Business Profile for around three years, but the profile was still struggling to achieve meaningful local search visibility. Despite having an established listing, the business was not consistently appearing within the Top 20 results for relevant local searches.

The objective was to identify the profile's visibility gaps, strengthen its local relevance and optimize the listing to improve its position in Google Maps and local search results.

Key Takeaways

Local visibility is built through consistency.

A Google Business Profile should not be treated as a one-time setup. Strong local presence comes from maintaining accurate information, publishing useful content, collecting genuine reviews and continuously improving the profile based on performance.

For Samruddhi T-shirt Dadar West, the focus was to create a stronger and more complete Google presence that supports discovery and customer actions.
