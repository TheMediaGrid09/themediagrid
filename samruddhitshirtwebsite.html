The Challenge

Good Products Need a Better Digital Presentation.

Samruddhi T-Shirts had products and a catalogue, but there wasn't a professional website where potential customers could properly explore the business and its offerings.

The challenge was not simply to put products online. The website needed to create a stronger first impression and make the catalogue easier to understand.

01

No Professional Website

The business did not have a dedicated professional website to represent its products and brand online.

02

Products Needed Better Presentation

Customers needed a clearer way to explore the available T-shirts, product categories and fabric options.

03

Catalogue Wasn't Digital

The product range needed to be converted into a structured digital catalogue that customers could browse easily.

The Solution

We Turned Their Catalogue Into a Digital Storefront.

Instead of creating a basic company website, we structured the experience around product discovery and catalogue presentation.

01

Product Catalogue

Products are organized into a structured catalogue so visitors can easily browse the available range.

02

Product Showcase

Product imagery and information are presented in a cleaner and more professional format.

03

Fabric Presentation

Fabric options and product characteristics can be communicated clearly to potential customers.

04

Mobile Responsive

Customers can browse the catalogue comfortably from smartphones, tablets and desktop devices.

05

Stronger Brand Presentation

The website gives the business a more professional digital identity when customers discover the brand online.

06

Easy Customer Enquiry

After exploring the catalogue, customers have a clear path toward making an enquiry.
