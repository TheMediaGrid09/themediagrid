FAQ

Questions about Google Business Profile optimization

What is Google Business Profile optimization?

It's the process of completing, correcting, and actively managing your Google listing — categories, photos, posts, and reviews — so it ranks higher and converts better.

How long does it take to see results?

Most businesses see initial movement in local rankings within 2–4 weeks, with stronger results compounding over 60–90 days.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. We work with local businesses across the US, UK, Canada, and Australia, communicating over email, WhatsApp, and video calls.

Is this a one-time service or ongoing?

Ongoing. Google rewards active, consistently updated profiles, so we optimize monthly rather than setting it up once and leaving it.

Will you also manage my reviews?

Yes — we set up a review generation strategy and respond to every review, positive or negative, on your behalf.

Do I need a website for this to work?

No, though it helps. GBP optimization drives value on its own — calls and directions don't require a website click-through.

What if my profile was suspended or flagged?

We review the cause, correct any policy issues, and guide the reinstatement process where possible.

Can you optimize multiple locations?

Yes, we manage single and multi-location profiles, keeping each one accurate and locally optimized.

How do you report results?

A monthly report covering calls, direction requests, profile views, and ranking movement — in plain English.

What does the free audit include?

A full review of your current profile against local ranking best practices, with the top issues costing you customers today.
