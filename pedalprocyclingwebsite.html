The Challenge

Too Many Calls for Questions the Website Could Answer.

Pedal Pro Cycling was dealing with repeated customer calls for basic information.

Customers were calling to ask questions such as rental prices, available options and other basic details that could have been communicated clearly online.

The problem wasn't a lack of customer interest. The problem was that customers had no simple digital place to get the answers themselves.

01

Repetitive Calls

Staff were spending time answering the same basic questions from customers who simply wanted to know rental information.

02

Pricing Wasn't Easily Accessible

Customers had to contact the business just to understand rental pricing instead of being able to compare options instantly.

03

Too Much Friction

Customers had to make an extra effort before they could decide whether the rental option was right for them.

The Solution

We Turned the Website Into a Self-Serve Information Hub.

Instead of forcing customers to call for basic information, the website was structured to answer the most common questions before the customer ever contacts the business.

01

Pricing Showcase

Rental prices are presented clearly so customers can understand the available options without calling the business.

02

Rental Options

Different rental options can be displayed in a structured format so customers can quickly understand what is available.

03

Information First

Important customer questions are answered directly through the website instead of relying entirely on phone conversations.

04

Mobile Experience

Customers can check pricing and rental information easily from their phones while planning their ride.

05

Clear Next Step

After reviewing the information, customers are given a clear path to contact or proceed with their rental enquiry.

06

Less Friction

The customer journey is designed to reduce unnecessary back-and-forth and make the decision-making process simpler.
