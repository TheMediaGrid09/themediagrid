The Challenge

Starting From Zero. Building Local Visibility.

Cycle Pro Hub needed a Google Business Profile to establish its presence on Google Search and Google Maps.

Instead of working with an existing profile, the project started from scratch. The objective was to create a complete and credible business presence, establish the correct local relevance and help the business begin appearing for relevant local searches.

Key Takeaways

A strong local presence starts with the right foundation.

Cycle Pro Hub did not begin with an established Google Business Profile. The project started from the ground up, creating the business's presence on Google and establishing the information required for local discovery.

The early ranking movement demonstrates why simply having a business listing is not enough. The profile needs to be correctly structured, relevant to the local market and consistently monitored as visibility develops.
