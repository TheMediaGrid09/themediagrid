FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

A few common questions about our website projects.

How long does a website take to build?

The timeline depends on the number of pages, content and functionality required. A straightforward business website can usually be completed much faster than a complex custom platform.

Will the website work on mobile?

Yes. Responsive design is part of the build so the website adapts to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can audit the existing website, identify the major problems and rebuild the experience around your current business requirements.

Can you connect WhatsApp to the website?

Yes. WhatsApp, calls and other enquiry pathways can be integrated into the website.

Will the website be SEO friendly?

We build a proper technical foundation including responsive structure, metadata and clean page architecture. Ongoing SEO is a separate growth activity.

Can you help after the website goes live?

Yes. Maintenance and ongoing improvements can be discussed depending on your requirements.
