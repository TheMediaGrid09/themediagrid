The Challenge

A Strong Local Business Needed a Stronger Google Presence.

Bike N Me operates in a highly competitive motorcycle helmet and riding-gear market in Mumbai. The objective was to improve the business's visibility when potential customers searched for helmets and related riding products nearby.

Instead of treating the Google Business Profile as a basic business listing, we focused on optimizing the profile around local relevance, customer discovery and actions.

Key Takeaways

A well-optimized GBP can turn local search into customer opportunities.

For a competitive category such as helmets and motorcycle riding gear, simply having a Google Business Profile is not enough. The profile needs to communicate relevance, location, products and trust clearly to both Google and potential customers.

Bike N Me's case demonstrates how a focused GBP optimization can improve local visibility and help a business compete more effectively for high-intent searches in Mumbai.
