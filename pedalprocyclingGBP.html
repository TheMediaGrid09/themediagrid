The Challenge

The Business Was There. The Location Wasn't Right.

Pedal Pro Cycling already had a Google Business Profile, but the location information was creating a problem for customers trying to find the business through Google Maps.

When a customer searches for a local cycling business, accurate location information is critical. An incorrect or poorly positioned location can create confusion, lead customers to the wrong place and reduce the usefulness of the Google Business Profile.

The objective was simple: identify the location issue and correct the Google Maps positioning so customers could find the business more accurately.

Key Takeaway

Sometimes the biggest GBP problem is not optimization.

A Google Business Profile is only useful if customers can accurately understand where the business is located. In Pedal Pro Cycling's case, the priority was not a large-scale SEO campaign. It was solving the fundamental location problem first.

By correcting the location representation, the profile became more useful for customers searching for and navigating to the business through Google Maps.
